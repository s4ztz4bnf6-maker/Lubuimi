A person or company may use, modify, and distribute covered code commercially if it complies with the AGPL, including the applicable source-code and network-interaction obligations. **The AGPL does not require an AGPL-compliant user to pay the author a royalty.** The license itself prohibits adding a fee or royalty for exercising the rights it grants.

The rights holder may separately offer the same original code under a **paid proprietary commercial license**. This alternative is intended for businesses that want to incorporate or distribute the code in closed-source products, avoid the AGPL's reciprocal obligations, or obtain negotiated enterprise permissions. This is dual licensing: AGPL for users who accept its terms, and a separately negotiated commercial license for customers who need different terms.

A commercial agreement may include:

- maintenance, updates, support, response-time commitments, and warranties;
- reporting, proportionate audit rights, territory, duration, and termination terms.

No price, royalty rate, minimum guarantee, or payment formula is fixed by this public policy. A paid license exists only when the parties expressly agree to its scope and terms in a signed written agreement.
