# Research update — 2026-10-26: infinite-test principle and 2-adic audit

## Scope and status

This note is a research direction for the Syracuse/Collatz formalization. It does not claim a proof of universal convergence or a counterexample. No local Lean build or CI run is claimed by this note.

## A key correction to the continuous-embedding idea

The standard Collatz map on the 2-adic integers can be defined by parity cases, since even and odd residue classes are clopen. That continuity alone does not imply that every positive-integer orbit reaches 1. The 2-adic topology measures divisibility, not ordinary magnitude: 2-adic convergence does not imply that an integer sequence decreases or remains bounded in the usual order. Compactness supplies convergent subsequences, not a global attracting fixed point.

For the accelerated odd map (T(n)=(3n+1)/2^{v_2(3n+1)}), valuation is locally constant on each cylinder where (v_2(3x+1)=k), but the valuation is unbounded across cylinders. The exceptional 2-adic point solving (3x+1=0) requires special treatment if extending the accelerated map to all of (ℤ_2). Therefore any proposed global 2-adic flow must specify its domain and prove continuity there; it cannot simply assume a smooth flow or a unique sink.

## Exact arithmetic interface already identified

For a genuine accelerated odd orbit with exponents (a_j=v_2(3n_j+1)), set (A_N=\sum_{j<N}a_j). The affine identity is
`2^{A_N} n_N = 3^N n_0 + C_N`,
where (C_0=0) and (C_{j+1}=3C_j+2^{A_j}).

The exact descent margin is
`M_N = 2^{A_N}n_0-(3^N n_0+C_N)=2^{A_N}(n_0-n_N)`.
Thus (M_N>0) is equivalent to strict descent; it is not an independent estimate. Any proof that derives (M_N>0) by assuming a bound equivalent to (n_N<n_0) is circular.

## Productive research split

Keep three claims separate:
1. **Algebraic identity:** the recurrence and affine identity, proved by induction.
2. **Realizability:** an exponent word ((a_0,\ldots,a_{N-1})) is generated by an actual orbit. Arbitrary positive exponent lists are not automatically realizable.
3. **Uniform descent mechanism:** every starting odd integer has some certified block with positive margin, or another global argument excludes all nontrivial cycles and divergent trajectories.

A useful next artifact is a transition certificate carrying (n_j), (a_j), and proofs of (2^{a_j}\mid 3n_j+1) and (2^{a_j+1}\nmid 3n_j+1). From such certificates, formalize the exact block identity and collect verified finite bounds. The hard theorem is a uniform argument over all starting values, not more finite examples.

## Infinite testing: the rigorous replacement

No physical computer executes an infinite test. The mathematical replacement is a proof that all states satisfy an invariant, a ranking function, or a well-founded descent relation. Finite model checking is complete only when the abstraction is proved to preserve the property; otherwise it is evidence or counterexample search, not a universal result.

For Collatz, a candidate ranking function must be proved to decrease on every required transition or on a rigorously certified block. Statistical drift, average multipliers, positive-density convergence, and large finite verification ranges do not exclude exceptional orbits.
