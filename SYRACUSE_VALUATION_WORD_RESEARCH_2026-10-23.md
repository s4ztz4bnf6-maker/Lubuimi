# Syracuse valuation-word research note — 2026-10-23

## Exact algebraic spine

For a certified odd trajectory with exponents a_j >= 1 and partial sums A_0=0, A_{j+1}=A_j+a_j, the exact affine identity is

  2^(A_N) x_N = 3^N x_0 + C_N,
  C_N = sum_{j<N} 3^(N-1-j) 2^(A_j).

The identity is formal algebra, not a descent theorem. A strict decrease is equivalent to C_N < (2^(A_N)-3^N) x_0. This separates two burdens: the exponent surplus 2^(A_N)-3^N must be positive, and it must dominate C_N/x_0. Proving only A_N log 2 > N log 3 handles the first burden, not the second.

## Proposed next formal object: realizable valuation words

Do not quantify over arbitrary words and then infer orbit behavior. Define a finite word certificate containing positive exponents a_j; partial sums A_j; odd states x_j; exact transition equations 2^(a_j) x_(j+1) = 3 x_j + 1; and an exactness witness that x_(j+1) is odd. The word is realizable only when these constraints are all satisfied. The next useful theorem is a congruence characterization of the possible next exponents after a fixed prefix, with modulus growing as 2^(A_N). Any global descent lemma must quantify over these realizable words, not all positive integer sequences.

4. Axiom reports and CI are reported separately from mathematical scope.


## Update after external frontier check (2026-10-23)

Two nearby developments were checked as boundary conditions, not imported as proof assumptions:

- A recent Collatz/Syracuse preprint reports an explicit positive-density subset of integers reaching 1 within a very large logarithmic bound. Positive density is not universality: a positive-density set can omit infinitely many integers, so this result cannot close the all-starting-values conjecture.
- A public conditional-cycle formalization states explicitly that its no-cycle theorem for up to 1322 odd terms uses hypotheses including a bound that is equivalent to the difficult large-cycle conclusion under its other assumptions. Its own erratum is a useful example of why hypotheses must be inspected semantically, not just through axiom reports.

The new local Lean increment on this branch is:
- `exact_quotient_formula_of_mod_eight_one`: for n = 8k+1, the exact quotient is 6k+1.
- `accelerated_descent_of_mod_eight_one`: for n = 8k+1 and n >= 2, the quotient is strictly smaller than n.
- Together with `exact_two_factors_of_mod_eight_one`, this is a fully accelerated valuation-two branch, not merely an intermediate quotient.

These results cover only one residue class. The remaining hard task is to prove a global block theorem whose hypotheses are generated by actual orbit transitions and whose cumulative offset is controlled. Neither a positive-density theorem nor average valuation heuristics supplies that pointwise block estimate.
