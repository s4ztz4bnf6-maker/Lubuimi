# Carry transfer matrices and a falsifiable entropy–Lyapunov interface (2026-10-27)

Status: exact algebraic research proposal. Not Lean-compiled; not a proof of Collatz. The transfer-matrix encoding is a new structure developed in this project, not a claim of historical priority.

## 1. The exact block as a matrix cocycle

For each positive integer valuation k, define
\[
M_k=\begin{pmatrix}3&1\\0&2^k\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its projective action on a rational x is
\[
x\longmapsto \frac{3x+1}{2^k}.
\]
For a valuation word \(w=(k_0,\ldots,k_{N-1})\), define the ordered product
\[
P(w)=M_{k_{N-1}}\cdots M_{k_0}.
\]
Writing \(A=\sum_{j<N}k_j\), direct induction gives
\[
P(w)=\begin{pmatrix}3^N&C_N\\0&2^A\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
C_0=0,\quad C_{j+1}=3C_j+2^{A_j}.
\]
Thus the entire block is encoded by two channels: the diagonal multiplier \(3^N/2^A\) and the off-diagonal carry \(C_N/2^A=q_N\). This is exact matrix multiplication, not a probabilistic approximation.

For an actual accelerated Syracuse orbit whose valuation word is w,
\[
n_N=\frac{3^N}{2^A}n_0+q_N,
\qquad
n_N<n_0\iff n_0\left(1-\frac{3^N}{2^A}\right)>q_N.
\]
The product matrix is therefore a compact, compositional certificate for block arithmetic.

## 2. The candidate Lyapunov interface

A useful candidate cannot merely be Shannon entropy of the valuation word. Entropy describes a distribution of words; Collatz requires a statement about every admissible orbit. Instead, associate to each finite word the exact signature
\[
\Sigma(w)=\left(N,A,\log_2(3^N/2^A),q_N\right).
\]
The first three coordinates measure multiplicative drift; the final coordinate measures the normalized additive carry. Under concatenation, the signatures compose by matrix multiplication. This avoids pretending that the two channels are independent.

A proposed block potential may take the form
\[
V(n,w)=\log_2 n-\log_2\!\left(\frac{3^N}{2^A}n+q_N\right),
\]
whenever the displayed terms are positive. For an actual orbit this equals \(\log_2(n_0/n_N)\). It is an exact descent score, but it is not yet a Lyapunov proof: it restates the block outcome. The research goal is to find a *local*, compositional bound on the carry channel that forces positive drift without already assuming descent.

## 3. The hard lemma, stated so it can fail

A theorem sufficient for progress would bound the carry coordinate for valuation words actually generated by integer orbits, in a way that implies infinitely many blocks satisfy
\[
2^A>3^N
\quad\text{and}\quad
C_N<(2^A-3^N)n_0.
\]
For an all-starting-value block theorem, the bound would have to be uniform in the admissible starting values. Arbitrary-word bounds are insufficient: the all-ones word \(k_j=1\) is realizable for arbitrarily long finite prefixes and gives growth.

A useful falsification target for any proposed entropy law is therefore the explicit family
\[
n_0=2^{N+1}m-1,\qquad n_j=3^j2^{N+1-j}m-1\quad(0\le j\le N),
\]
with \(k_j=1\) for \(j<N\). It has \(n_N>n_0\). Any claimed universal critical-length descent must fail this test.

## 4. Why the acoustic analogy needs mathematics

One can interpret the valuation word as a signal and \(P(w)\) as its exact transfer operator. Spectral radius or average-symbol entropy may help discover candidate inequalities, but neither alone constrains every deterministic admissible word. Any signal-processing or spectral claim must be converted into an inequality for the ordered matrix product and its off-diagonal term. The matrix order matters; replacing the word by symbol counts loses carry information.

## 5. Lean proof ladder

1. Define \(M_k\) as a 2×2 matrix over \(\mathbb N\) or \(\mathbb Z\), and define the product recursively to avoid list-order ambiguity.
2. Prove the product formula \(P(w)_{11}=3^N\), \(P(w)_{22}=2^A\), and \(P(w)_{12}=C_N\) by induction.
3. Prove the projective/block identity after separately proving the denominator is positive.
4. Prove the descent equivalence with cross-multiplication over integers; avoid silently relying on natural subtraction.
5. Formalize the all-ones counterfamily and use it as a regression test against every proposed universal fixed-block theorem.
6. Only then add a genuinely new global bound, with no entropy-independence assumption. Run the project’s pinned Lean build and transitive axiom audit before calling any result formally verified.

## Current conclusion

The matrix cocycle improves the *language* of the obstruction and makes concatenation exact. It does not yet bound admissible valuation words, prove divergence impossible, or prove convergence. The missing mathematical content remains a deterministic orbitwise carry/drift theorem.
